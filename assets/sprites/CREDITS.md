No licence file ships with it; the author states the terms in a comment on that page (https://itch.io/post/3305230): *"This is a completely free asset, use it in your games! However do not resell this asset in any form of graphical bundle (as in reselling the sprite sheets), also, you do not have to put me in the credits."* Credited anyway. Modified: recoloured to the palette (10 colours → 5; the ramp order is preserved), 16 frames flattened into one 256×16 strip (idle 0–1, fly 2–9, walk 10–12, eat 13–15).
- **Palette:** Gooseberry Ghost (8) + one bone-shadow swatch `#cdcd99` + a violet ramp `#5e2d8c` `#8a4fb5` `#c79df2` (intrusive thoughts / kikis).

No generative art was used anywhere in this project.
