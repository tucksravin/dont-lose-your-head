Flagging this interpretation explicitly: if the team wants panic tied strictly to move-key input, that's a one-line change in `PanicCounter._physics_process` (check `Input.get_axis(...)` instead of `_body.velocity`), but velocity keeps `PanicCounter` decoupled from input-action names and works the same regardless of what's driving `Body`.
